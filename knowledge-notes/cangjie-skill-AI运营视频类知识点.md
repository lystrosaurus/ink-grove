## kangarooking/ai-for-everyone-skill
- 来源：吴恩达《AI for Everyone》视频课程 — 25 skill
- 归类：AI 可行性判断 / 项目选择与评估 / 项目执行流程 / 组织转型与数据战略 / 战略与行动
- 真实 skill 清单：
  - AI 可行性：`ab-mapping`(A→B映射)、`one-second-rule`(一秒法则)、`ml-feasibility`(双因素框架)
  - 项目选择：`cross-functional-brainstorming`、`automate-task`(自动化任务非岗位)、`triple-due-diligence`(技术/商业/伦理尽调)、`three-value-drivers`(降本/增收/新业务)
  - 执行流程：`ml-vs-ds`、`ml-workflow`、`train-test-split`、`statistical-acceptance`、`ai-pipeline`
  - 组织转型：`ai-transformation-playbook`(五步)、`pilot-momentum-flywheel`、`role-tiered-training`、`ai-team-building`、`data-flywheel`、`unified-data-warehouse`、`dont-wait-perfect-data`、`dont-acquire-for-data`
  - 战略：`build-vs-buy`、`ai-strategy-moat`、`iterate-not-perfect`、`start-small-find-partner`、`ai-project-lifecycle`

## kangarooking/loop-engineering-skill

- 真实 skill 清单：
